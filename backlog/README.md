## How this layer works

- **Release plan** defines the trajectory from S0 to GA.
- **Risk register** tracks anything that can derail that trajectory.
- **NFR register** is the measurement view of architectural commitments.

All three are reviewed bi-weekly during build, weekly during pilot.
